This is the KOOS-JR (Knee Injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score – Joint Replacement) Arabic version. It contains 7 questions: 1 stiffness, 4 pain, and 2 function questions. KOOS-JR-Saudi-Arabic.pdf

Step 1: Assign a score to each answer

For every question:

* No symptoms / No difficulty = 0
* Mild = 1
* Moderate = 2
* Severe = 3
* Extreme = 4

Step 2: Add the scores

Sum the scores of the 7 items.

* Minimum raw score = 0 (best possible knee)
* Maximum raw score = 28 (worst possible knee)

Step 3: Convert to the KOOS-JR score (0–100)

The KOOS-JR uses a conversion table (Rasch transformation), not a simple percentage. The converted score ranges from:

* 100 = no knee disability
* 0 = worst knee disability
